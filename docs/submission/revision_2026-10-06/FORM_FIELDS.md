**Project Title:**
Liver and Heart Footprint of Indian Recipes: A Computational Gastronomy Framework for Estimating and Reducing the Cardiometabolic Dietary Load of Indian Food

**Description:**
Fatty liver disease (MASLD) affects an estimated 38.9% of Indian adults, and dietary risks account for 38.5% of cardiovascular deaths in India. Their shared dietary drivers (saturated fat, added sugar, refined carbohydrate, sodium, excess energy and oil absorbed in frying) are built into everyday Indian recipes, yet no tool tells a cook how much a given recipe adds to this load.

We define the liver and heart footprint of a recipe, analogous to the carbon footprint of recipes: recipe → ingredients × quantities × cooking process → per-serving nutrient load, expressed as a share of ICMR-NIN 2024 / WHO daily limits. The estimate is grounded in Indian food composition data (IFCT 2017 via the Indian Nutrient Databank).

The project delivers:

1. a dataset of laboratory-measured fat in Indian fried foods;
2. the computation engine;
3. a footprint atlas of Indian recipes;
4. a web application.

Each deliverable has a stated verification. Outputs are dietary estimates, not medical advice.

**Deliverable 1:**
Indian Fried-Food Fat Dataset.

- **What:** a CSV of at least 60 laboratory-measured fat values of Indian fried foods from at least 5 published sources, each row citing its source table, plus fat ranges (minimum, median, maximum) for 5 fried-food categories (battered fritters, flat doughs, stuffed pastries, crisp namkeen, syrup-soaked sweets).
- **Verification:** every row traces to its source. A script recomputes the ranges and reports how often a held-out source's values fall inside the range built from the other sources.

**Deliverable 2:**
Recipe Liver and Heart Load Engine.

- **What:** a tool that takes a recipe's ingredient list and returns, per serving, energy, total fat, saturated fat, added sugar, sodium and refined carbohydrate as a percentage of ICMR-NIN 2024 / WHO daily limits, shown as a Liver view and a Heart view, with every value traced to its ingredient lines. Fried dishes use the Deliverable 1 ranges.
- **Verification:**
  - reproduces the published Indian Nutrient Databank values for at least 95% of its about 1,000 dishes;
  - parsing and gram-conversion accuracy is reported on at least 150 hand-annotated real ingredient lines;
  - estimates for at least 10 laboratory-analysed cooked dishes are compared with measured values.

**Deliverable 3:**
Liver and Heart Footprint Atlas of Indian Recipes.

- **What:** footprints of at least 1,000 Indian Nutrient Databank recipes (textbook) and at least 1,000 recipes from at least 3 Indian recipe websites, covering:
  - load by dish category;
  - the contribution of ingredient groups (cooking oils, dairy fats, sugar, refined flour, salt) to saturated fat, added sugar and sodium;
  - a textbook versus web comparison for at least 20 dishes present in both.
- **Verification:** all tables and figures are generated by scripts from saved results, with the share of recipes and ingredient mass analysed reported.

**Deliverable 4:**
Web Application.

- **What:** a locally run web app where a user pastes an ingredient list or a link from at least 3 supported recipe websites and sees the per-serving Liver and Heart bars, a per-ingredient table with sources, and the top contributing ingredients.
- **Verification:** demonstrated live on at least 10 recipe links. The values shown equal the engine's output for the same recipe.
