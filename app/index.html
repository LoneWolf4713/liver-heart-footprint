How frying.py Uses Settings
Exact lookup & transparent fallback

In D4, users can toggle cooking environments (e.g. Street vs. Restaurant) to see how oil uptake shifts:

  1. Match dish rows: Base name lookup gathers all matching entries (e.g. 5 Samosa rows).
  2. Filter by setting: If entries match the chosen setting (e.g. Samosa + street → Gupta 2016: 29.4 g/100g, 54.7% SFA), the engine takes their median.
  3. Transparent fallback: If no exact row exists for that setting (e.g. Samosa has no home row), it falls back to the median of all matched rows (12.4 g/100g) and flags fallback_used=True.
Why Lab Studies Differ: Three Analytical Extraction Protocols

These protocols come directly from the 10 source papers, explaining why Soxhlet and gas-chromatography (FAME-sum) measurements separate:

🧪 Soxhlet Extraction
21 rows
Gupta 2016 · Street vendors
Continuous solvent extraction (ether / hexane). Captures all extractable neutral lipids, yielding higher fat readings (e.g. 29.6 g street samosa) plus SFA%.
🔬 Proximate Analysis
33 rows
UK CoFID · safefood · Controlled Trials
AOAC acid hydrolysis + ether extraction used by national food agencies. Reliable total fat, though agency tables and trials often omit fatty-acid SFA%.
🧬 FAME-Sum GC-FID
15 rows
Joshee 2019 · Fatty-acid profile
Sums identified fatty-acid methyl ester peaks. Gives exact SFA%, but runs systematically lower on total fat (e.g. 7.7 g samosa) because non-FA lipids aren't summed.

Stage 01 src/lhf/parse.py
Text Standardization

Ingredient Line Parser

Extracts structured quantity, canonical unit, size, embedded weights, and clean food name across 27 canonical units (English + Hindi/Devanagari).
  • Fractions & Ranges: Evaluates Unicode fractions (½ → 0.5), English number words ("one"–"ten"), and midpoint ranges ("1-2 tsp" → 1.5).
  • Embedded Metric Priority: Captures explicit parenthetical weights/volumes (stated_g, stated_ml) first so metric values override imprecise cup guesses.
  • Explicit Status Tagging: Labels every line as QUANTIFIED, UNQUANTIFIED (e.g. "salt to taste"), or WATER (always excluded).

Stage 02 src/lhf/match.py
Database Lookup (Runs Before Weighing)

Composition Database Matching

Matches the food name first — against 746 unified nutrition entries — so Stage 03 knows the exact food-specific cup/piece weight.
  • 746 Unified Food Rows: Combines 542 IFCT 2017 Indian foods with 144 INDB UK, 54 INDB US, and 6 Indian retail product labels (L901–L906: Amul milk, dahi, paneer, butter, cream, malai).
  • Culinary Priority Rules + 4,179 Aliases: Resolves Indian terms deterministically (paneer → L903, ghee → T013, generic oil → T508 sunflower oil, chicken → N500).
  • Strict Fuzzy Cutoff (0.85): Uses difflib.SequenceMatcher with FUZZY_MATCH_CUTOFF = 0.85; anything below stays UNMAPPED instead of guessing wrong.

Stage 03 grams.py · compute.py · classify.py
Weighing, Nutrient Sum & Class Tagging

Unit-to-Grams Conversion & Nutrient Arithmetic

1 cup of maida (120g) weighs half as much as 1 cup of cubed paneer (246g). Resolves grams via 243 curated measure rules, sums 12 nutrients, and tags risk classes.
  • Measure Cascade (measures.csv): Applies STATED_MASS → STATED_VOLUME (using specific density, e.g. 0.92 g/ml for oil) → CODE_MEASURE (food + unit + size) → GROUP_MEASURE → ANY_MEASURE.
  • Functional Class Tagging (classes.csv): Tags lines as DAIRY_FAT, COOKING_OIL, HYDROGENATED_FAT, ADDED_SUGAR, REFINED_GRAIN, SALT, or SPICE (so natural tomato sugar isn't miscounted as added sugar!).

Stage 04 · Core Correction src/lhf/frying.py
Lab-Anchored Oil Absorption

Frying Bath Detection & Lab Substitution

Excludes the unconsumed pan oil bath and substitutes empirically measured absorbed fat from fried_foods_lab.csv (with optional cooking setting & fallback).
  • Bath Trigger: Flags any COOKING_OIL or HYDROGENATED_FAT line ≥ 50g containing a frying phrase ("for deep frying", "to fry"), while ignoring non-fried dishes (biryani, curry).
  • Exclude Bath, Inject Uptake: Removes 100% of the bath's fat & calories (FRYING_BATH) and injects (non-oil mass / 100) × lab_fat_g_100g using the matched dish (and selected setting, with automatic dish fallback if missing).

Per-Serving 24h Budget Footprint

Scoring Against ICMR-NIN & WHO Daily Limits

Divides totals by servings and maps 6 independent axes against full-day adult limits — with zero arbitrary composite score weights.
  • Liver Load Axes: Saturated Fat (<10%E), Added Free Sugar (25g/day), Refined Starches (raw grams + % dish kcal, no official cap), and Calories (2,240 kcal urban).
  • Heart Load Axes: Saturated Fat, Sodium (1,967 mg/day from 5g salt), Total Fat (30%E), and Calories.
  • Honest Guardrails: Automatically emits warnings for SALT_UNQUANTIFIED (lower bound), SPICE_OVER_LIMIT (>30g), MASS_IMPLAUSIBLE, and NO_SERVINGS.

Data Sources, Limitations & Credits

Empirical Data Sources & Academic Bibliography

1. Unified Composition Table (746 rows): 542 entries from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), plus 144 INDB UK, 54 INDB US, and 6 Indian retail dairy labels (L901–L906).

2. INDB (Indian Nutrient Databank): Standardized corpus of 1,014 traditional recipes providing our D3 culinary baseline.

3. ICMR-NIN DGI 2024 & NSO HCES 2022–24: Establishes SFA ceilings (<10% general, <7% high-risk), total fat (30%E), sugar (25g), salt (5g), and 2,240 kcal urban baseline.

4. Gupta et al. (2016): Soxhlet lipid extraction of 21 Delhi street foods across village and urban samples.

5. UK CoFID (2021) & safefood (2015): Proximate lab analyses for Indian home, restaurant takeaway, and retail snacks.

6. Joshee (2019), Neethu (2025) & Controlled Trials: GC-FID fatty acid profiles and controlled frying studies (Chen 2009, Sakhale 2011, Yadav 2012, Gaikwad 2022, Jain 2024).

7. ICMR-INDIAB-21 (Nat Med 2025, n=18,090) & Phenome India (2026): National dietary fat/carb intake and 38.9% Indian adult MASLD prevalence.

Acknowledged Model Limitations & Boundary Conditions

• Confection Frying Mass Distortion: In syrup-soaked sweets (Gulab Jamun, Jalebi), finished food mass includes heavy sugar syrup, inflating the mass multiplier in absorbed fat re-injection (~5% of corrected recipes).

• Unquantified Culinary Salt: Recipes listing "salt to taste" lack gram weights, so reported sodium is an empirical lower bound from intrinsic ingredients only.

• INDB Dry Stock-Cube Soup Coding: 26 of 47 INDB soups (including BFP066, BFP067, and ASC079) code stock volume (480 ml or 0.5 cup) directly against dry concentrate cubes (K506/K507), inflating the Soups & Stews atlas median to 1,028.3% of daily sodium.

• Schema.org Scraper Coverage: Planned URL scraping targets structured JSON-LD (schema.org/Recipe), supported on major Indian portals (~70% coverage).
