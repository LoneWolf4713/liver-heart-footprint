The Range Problem in Dietary Epidemiology
The same culinary dish exhibits up to a 4× lipid spread across real-world settings. For example, Samosa total fat ranges from 7.71 g/100g (gas chromatography FAME-sum in restaurant takeaway) to 29.62 g/100g (Soxhlet extraction in village street food, Gupta 2016). Both are valid empirical figures: thermal oxidation, repeated frying cycles, and batter porosity fundamentally alter lipid retention. Our pipeline matches recipes by specific dish classification and setting, falling back to category medians.

MASLD Burden (Liver Axis)
Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) affects 38.9% of Indian adults (Lancet Regional Health SE Asia 2026). The dietary etiology is driven by rapid hepatic de novo lipogenesis stimulated by saturated fats, added sugars, and refined starches.
Cardiovascular Burden (Heart Axis)
Cardiovascular disease (CVD) contributes to 28% of total mortality in India (Global Burden of Disease). National SFA consumption averages 8.9% of energy (11.2% in North India), while sodium intake exceeds 8 g/day against the 5 g ceiling.

Empirical Data Sources & References

Ground Truth

1. IFCT 2017: Indian Food Composition Tables, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR-NIN). 1,003 primary food components with proximate and lipid profile assays.

2. INDB: Indian Nutrient Databank. 1,014 standardized traditional recipes providing the textbook baseline corpus.

3. ICMR-NIN 2024: Dietary Guidelines for Indians. SFA ceilings (<10% general, <5% high-risk), total fat ceiling (30% energy), and 2,240 kcal urban baseline.

4. Gupta et al. (2016): Soxhlet lipid extraction and fatty acid profiling of cooked foods and street snacks available in Delhi.

5. UK CoFID (2021): Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset. Proximate lab values for ethnic restaurant and takeaway portions.

6. Neethu et al. (2025): Controlled deep immersion frying trials of traditional milk-solid sweets (Gulab Jamun / Pantoa) in ghee vs vegetable oil.

7. ICMR-INDIAB (Nature Medicine 2025): Cross-sectional epidemiological study (n=18,090) reporting national SFA consumption patterns.

Acknowledged Model Limitations

Boundary Conditions

• Confection Frying Mass Distortion: In syrup-soaked sweets (Gulab Jamun, Jalebi), finished food mass includes sugar syrup, which inflates the mass multiplier used in absorbed fat re-injection. Affects ~5% of corrected recipes.

• Unquantified Culinary Salt: Recipes listing "salt to taste" lack explicit gram weights. Sodium values for these dishes represent strict empirical lower bounds.

• Pot-Level INDB Soup Coding: Soups & Stews in INDB are coded at the preparation batch level rather than per-bowl service size, causing anomalous sodium figures in the raw atlas.

• Schema.org Web Scraper Coverage: URL scraping requires structured JSON-LD (`schema.org/Recipe`). Supported on major portals (~70% compliance across Indian recipe sites).
