# Liver and Heart Footprint of Indian Recipes
BIO544 Computational Gastronomy mini-project, IIIT-Delhi (Prof. Ganesh Bagler), Monsoon 2026. Group 45: Parth Verma (2024407), Pratyaksh Kumar (2024431).

Paste an Indian recipe link and get its per-serving liver and heart footprint (saturated fat, trans fat, added sugar, refined carbohydrate, energy, sodium vs ICMR-NIN 2024 / WHO limits and the average Indian's intake), an explanation of what drives it, and authentic lighter alternatives. It is backed by an open dataset of fried-food fat and a cuisine-level study.
